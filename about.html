About — SoftBoard

Built from South Africa, for physics that looks the same everywhere.

SoftBoard is the company behind PDIS-16. We're building infrastructure monitoring that a working engineer would recognise — grounded in the equations already used to design pipes, structures and power systems, applied continuously instead of once at commissioning. This page is about who's building it and where it honestly stands today.

Why we build this way.

Most infrastructure monitoring answers the wrong question. It asks "does this reading look unusual compared to the past?" — a question that needs history, tuning, and a model nobody fully trusts. PDIS-16 asks a harder but more honest one: "does this reading violate the physics of the asset?" That question has a fixed answer that doesn't drift with the training set, and it's meaningful the very first day a sensor comes online.

A pipe under pressure behaves like physics, not geography. That's the whole thesis: the same equations hold in Johannesburg, Rotterdam or Houston, so a system built on them travels without being retrained for each site.

What we're building toward, plainly.
