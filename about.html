About

SoftBoard is the company behind PDIS-16.

Based in South Africa — but physics doesn't have borders. A pipe under pressure behaves the same in Johannesburg, Rotterdam or Houston, so a system built on physical law travels without being rebuilt for each site.

Why we build this way

We don't ask what's unusual. We ask what's physically possible.

Most monitoring compares a reading to the past — and needs history, tuning, and a model nobody fully trusts. PDIS-16 asks a harder, more honest question: does this reading obey the physics of the asset? That answer is fixed, doesn't drift with a training set, and is meaningful the first day a sensor comes online.

Where it stands

A working, proven system — not a concept.

PDIS-16 is fully developed and operational: a built, working system that runs continuous physics against live telemetry and integrates into existing SCADA and PLC environments. Our mission is simple — engineer deterministic software that bridges industrial automation with real-time physical truth, and prevent catastrophic drift in critical assets before it becomes failure.
